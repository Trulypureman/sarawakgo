1 · Estimation tool only

SarawakGo is a free planning tool for trips to Sarawak, Malaysia. Flight fares, hotel rates, taxes and daily spending figures are reference estimates based on average market data. They are not live prices.

SarawakGo does not connect to any airline or hotel reservation system. Only the weather forecast (Open-Meteo) and exchange rates (Frankfurter, ECB reference rates) are fetched live.
